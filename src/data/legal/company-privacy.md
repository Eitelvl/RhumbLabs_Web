# Company Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** June 9, 2026

At Rhumb Labs, we value your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This privacy policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information across our corporate website, communication channels, and general products list.

## Scope of this Policy
This General Privacy Policy outlines our corporate practices regarding:
- **Corporate Website & Communication:** Data collected when you visit our website, submit requests via our contact forms, or email our team.
- **Product Hub:** General privacy standards maintained by Rhumb Labs as the parent developer of our leading navigation and tracking software products, including **RhumbNav** and **Pogo**.

For specific, technical privacy details of our individual applications—including flight navigation logs, location tracking, and sync data—please refer to the dedicated legal documentation of each product, accessible via our web-based Legal Center.

## Information We Collect
We may collect personal information such as your name, email address, and other contact details when you interact with our website, subscribe to newsletters, or contact our support team. We also collect non-personal data through cookies and analytics tools to improve our website experience.

## How We Use Your Information
- To provide customer support and maintain our corporate services.
- To communicate with you regarding updates, support, and promotional offers (if you have opted in).
- To analyze website traffic and user behavior in order to improve our digital products and website.
- To comply with legal obligations.

## Data Sharing and Disclosure
We do *not* sell your personal data to third parties. We may share your information with trusted service providers who assist us in operating our website and conducting our business, provided they agree to keep your data confidential.

## Data Security
We implement standard security measures to protect your personal data from unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure. However, no internet transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## Your Rights
Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data. You may also have the right to opt-out of certain data processing activities. Please contact us to exercise these rights.

## Policy Updates
We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Effective Date."

## Contact Us
For privacy-related inquiries, please contact us at [support@rhumblabs.com](mailto:support@rhumblabs.com) or through our website's contact form.
